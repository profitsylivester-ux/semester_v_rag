# GSU 07512 — Business Management in ICT

## 1. Management functions (POLC)
Planning → Organising → Leading → Controlling. Mintzberg roles: interpersonal, informational, decisional.

## 2. Business models & strategy
- Canvas blocks: value proposition, segments, channels, revenue, costs, key resources/partners.
- Porter 5 forces: new entrants, substitutes, buyer/supplier power, rivalry.
- SWOT → TOWS matrix turns findings into actions.

| Strategy | Focus | Telco example |
|---|---|---|
| Cost leadership | Lowest cost/scale | Bulk data bundles |
| Differentiation | Unique quality | Best rural coverage |
| Focus | Niche segment | Enterprise MPLS only |

## 3. Project management
- Triple constraint: scope–time–cost (+quality).
- PERT expected time $T_E = (O + 4M + P)/6$; critical path = longest path (zero float).
- EVM: $CV = EV - AC$, $SV = EV - PV$, $CPI = EV/AC$, $SPI = EV/PV$.

## 4. Finance basics
- NPV $= \sum CF_t/(1+r)^t - C_0$; accept if NPV $> 0$. IRR is the $r$ with NPV $= 0$.
- Break-even $Q = FC/(P - VC)$. Depreciation: straight-line $(Cost - Salvage)/Life$.

## 5. Solved problem
Project: $FC = 50{,}000$, $P = 100$, $VC = 60$ → $Q = 50{,}000/(100-60) = 1{,}250$ units to break even.

## 6. ICT governance & ethics
- ITIL service lifecycle; COBIT control objectives; data-privacy principles (consent, minimisation, security).
- E-waste handling and energy-aware networks as CSR items.
